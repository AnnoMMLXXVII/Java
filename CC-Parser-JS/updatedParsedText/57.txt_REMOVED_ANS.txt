3. A nurse teaches a client with Cushing disease. Which dietary requirements would the nurse include in this clients health teaching? ( Select all that apply. ) A nurse teaches a client with Cushing disease. Which dietary requirements would the nurse include in this clients health teaching? ( Select all that apply. )
a. Low calcium
b. Low carbohydrate
c. Low protein
d. Low calories
e. Low sodium

[ANS : REMOVED]

The client with Cushing disease has weight gain, muscle loss, hyperglycemia, and sodium retention. Dietary modifications need to include reduction of carbohydrates and total calories to prevent or reduce the degree of hyperglycemia. Sodium retention causes water retention and hypertension. Clients are encouraged to restrict their sodium intake moderately. Clients 
often have bone density loss and need more calcium. Increased protein intake will help decrease muscle loss.
