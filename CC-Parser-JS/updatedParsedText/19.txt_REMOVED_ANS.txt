1. The nurse learning about cellular regulation understands that which process occurs during the S phase of the cell cycle? The nurse learning about cellular regulation understands that which process occurs during the S phase of the cell cycle?
a. Actual division (mitosis) 
b. Doubling of DNA
c. Growing extra membrane
d. No reproductive activity 

[ANS : REMOVED]

During the S phase, the cell must double its DNA content through DNA synthesis. Actual division, or mitosis, occurs during the M phase. Growing extra membrane occurs in the G1
phase. During the G0 phase, the cell is working but is not involved in any reproductive activity.

6. The nurse caring for oncology clients knows that which form of metastasis is the most common? The nurse caring for oncology clients knows that which form of metastasis is the most common?
a. Bloodborne
b. Direct invasion
c. Lymphatic spread
d. Via bone marrow

[ANS : REMOVED]

Bloodborne metastasis is the most common way for cancer to metastasize. Direct invasion and lymphatic spread are other methods. Bone marrow is not a medium in which cancer spreads, although cancer can occur in the bone marrow.
